      Metro

      DXB, the gateway to Dubai adventure, provides direct access to one of the world's most modern, clean, and efficient transit systems: the Dubai Metro. Offering air-conditioned comfort and cost-effectiveness, it's the ideal option for exploring the city. 
      Operational hours
      The Dubai Metro's Red Line offers connections from DXB Terminals 1 and 3, operating: 

      Monday to Thursday: 5AM - midnight
      Friday: 5AM - 1AM (next day)
      Saturday: 5AM - midnight
      Sunday: 8AM - midnight

      Please note, service hours may extend during public holidays and citywide celebrations.
      For the latest timings, always check the RTA website.

      Journey planning
      Dubai Metro is easy to navigate, with two lines – Red and Green – offering seamless connections to buses, taxis, major hotels, and key attractions across the city. Guests can plan their journey using the RTA’s s’hail application.
      Ticket information
      Metro Red line passes are available at ticket offices and vending machines in DXB Terminals 1 and 3. For added convenience, guests may purchase a Nol Card, available in different types (Gold and Silver). For more information on fares and travel zones, please click here.
      Luggage policy
      Travel light with up to two pieces of luggage, including hand luggage. For detailed information on luggage size and weight restrictions, visit the RTA website.
      Reaching DWC
      Guests can connect to DWC via RTA buses or taxis from Ibn Battuta and Expo 2020 Metro Stations. Journey planning is available through the RTA’s s’hail application.
